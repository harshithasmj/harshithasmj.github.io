## Data Science

### Stress-Testing Organizational Stability
A scenario model that makes human capital risk measurable. It estimates how workforce attrition, leadership turnover, and revenue pressure affect an organization's financial exposure, and converts that into a 0-100 Stability Score with recommended intervention thresholds.

- Compares three scenarios: Base, Moderate Stress, and Severe Stress
- Models workforce replacement cost, leadership instability cost, and revenue shock
- **Key insight:** the organization is highly sensitive to even moderate workforce volatility, which suggests retention and leadership continuity are major stabilizing factors

**Tools:** Python, Jupyter
[View on GitHub](https://github.com/harshithasmj/Stress-Testing-Organizational-Stability)

### Employee Attrition Prediction
A machine learning model that identifies employees at risk of leaving, using structured HR data, so retention conversations can be targeted early.

- Cleaned and encoded the data, and handled class imbalance with weighted models
- Compared Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest
- Evaluated with precision, recall, and F1-score rather than accuracy alone
- **Findings:** overtime was the strongest predictor of attrition, lower tenure and income raised risk, and Random Forest gave the best balance of recall and precision

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn
[View on GitHub](https://github.com/harshithasmj/Employee-Attrition)
